# Stage 7 UX Blueprint — LiSN (Retail / e-commerce) / Category-Business Head

> Phase 3, stage 4 of 5. Inputs: Stage 6 UI spec (five screens S1–S5) + Stage 4 personas + Stage 5 model (C-/O- codes, signal blocks) + the Fluid/LiSN card-anatomy. Output turns the static screen set into flows, **distinct** drill-down signatures, the dual-action ordering on shared cards, the process-gap diamond, the ordered storyline, interaction states, and the human-gate moments. Every flow traverses the domain spine end to end: **Interaction → Signal → Business Issue → Owner → Evidence → Recommended Action.** Brand rules applied; every AI element carries the ✦ marker.

## 2. Distinct drill-down signatures (no two card types share a drill — a hard LiSN preference)

Each card *type* gets the drill its job demands; the shape comes from the methodology card-anatomy and what the card must prove.

- **A · Recoverable-margin Signal (T2-02) — "decomposition" drill.** Ranked cause-code bars → the **fixable-vs-intent split** (the GoKwik ~60–70%-intent prior shown as the trust anchor) → the specific PIM attribute at fault → the verbatim cluster → ruled-out (buyer-remorse held out, sale-day lift, warehouse mis-pick rerouted to S4). *How it differs:* it decomposes a rate into recoverable vs unrecoverable — the only drill answering "how much of this can I actually get back".
- **B · Lane arbitration verdict (T2-26) — "adjudication" drill.** A two-way voice-theme split (delivery-theme vs product-theme) with the deciding share → a verdict banner (**logistics** or **seller**) → the process-gap branch. *Differs:* a binary owner adjudication with a confidence-banded verdict; its job is to assign blame correctly, not to size a number.
- **C · Festival real-vs-failure verdict (T2-28) — "verification" drill.** The spike plotted against the **sale-scaled baseline** → the failure-voice corroboration timeline → the aligned account/fraud signal → the **conservative tier selector** with a confidence band; the **suppressed near-miss** sits inline (an expected-demand spike shown as suppressed). *Differs:* an "is this even real" check against a moving baseline whose default verdict can be *suppress* — unique in showing a non-event.
- **D · Aspect-sentiment cliff (T2-12) — "leading-indicator" drill.** The aspect's negative-share **slope vs its own trailing mix** (explicitly NOT the star average) → overlaid with the conversion/return co-movement → presented as a **correlation-evidence band** (directional, not proven cause). *Differs:* a slope/early-warning drill that wears its correlation honesty on the face.
- **E · Promo health composite (T2-19) — "gate" drill.** A composite gauge decomposed into its inputs (return band · sentiment slope · availability · seller health) shown **against ROAS** → resolving to promote / caution / do-not. *Differs:* a multi-input gate that weighs a tempting good metric (ROAS) against bad ones; its job is to justify a "no" to a "yes".
- **F · Seller Trust-Risk Board / OwnershipBoard (T2-07) — "portfolio → contributor" drill.** Ranked seller rows → click a row → that seller's breakdown (complaint clusters · repeat-contact · SLA breach · concentration band vs the 25% cap) → the per-seller evidence pack. *Differs:* starts from a ranked portfolio and drills to one contributor's evidence — a coverage card, not a single Signal.
- **G · Seller-dispute triage (T2-11) — "worklist" drill.** A short item list → one dispute's detail with evidence both ways → uphold / reverse / coach. *Differs:* an event-triggered accept-or-route queue, distinct from the always-on Signal drills.
- **H · Defect-wave (T2-15) — "early-warning" drill.** A return-initiation spike co-moving with a care-transcript defect theme, on the real-time tier → an early-recall route. *Differs:* pairs a substrate spike with a transcript theme *before* returns complete — a co-movement drill living on the real-time tier.

Routing is by each card's own cohort/ID, never a shared constant (a known bug class — a heatmap/board cell must route by its own key).

## 3. Dual-action ordering on shared cards (the two-buyer design)

T2-02, T2-12, and T2-26 are read by **both** Priya (Category) and the CX/VoC Head, with different first actions. Rule: **the screen owner-of-record sets the primary button; the peer action is always visible but never the default on the other persona's screen.** On a persona switch the ordering flips (and the rail re-ranks).

| Shared card | Category Head primary (Priya's screen) | CX/VoC Head action (secondary here; primary on CX screen) |
|---|---|---|
| **T2-02** recoverable margin | **Draft PIM sizing-chart fix** + **Route fixable share to Seller-Brand** | Pre-empt the size-guide ticket / proactive comms to the at-risk cohort |
| **T2-12** aspect cliff | **Draft catalogue / quality fix on the aspect** | Brief care on the aspect + update canned responses before the ticket wave |
| **T2-26** lane arbitration | **Route lane verdict to the resolved owner** (Ops/Seller) | Draft proactive delivery-failure comms to the affected cohort |

Showing both buttons (one primary, one ghost) is itself a demo beat — it makes the "one join, two buyers" story visible without leaving the screen.

## 4. Cross-owner / process-gap "diamond" (where interaction intelligence reveals a process owner's gap)

Designed explicitly, never implicit. Each is cohort-level, drafted, and human-gated.

| Trigger (interaction reveals) | Routed owner / process map | What they receive |
|---|---|---|
| "Wrong item shipped" return free-text co-moving with a **pick/pack exception** (T2-04 / S4) | **Operations — warehouse** process map | the cohort, the exception pattern, the verbatims, a drafted process-fix note — **not** a seller penalty |
| A **sizing** free-text cluster localised to a single PIM attribute (T2-03 / S2) | **Catalogue / PIM** process map | the attribute at fault, a drafted attribute remap, the verbatim cluster |
| "Rider didn't attempt / marked undelivered" lane voice (T2-26 / S4) | **Operations — last-mile** process | the lane, the deciding voice share, the resolved orders, a drafted lane-ops escalation |

The diamond is the demo's highest-value cross-owner moment: a return that *looked* like a seller fault is shown to be a warehouse process gap, routed to the right map, with the seller penalty visibly held.

## 5. Demo storyline (ordered beats — built from the most differentiating first; each ends in act-now + approve)

- **Beat 0 · The Monday view (S1).** *Hook:* Priya opens the Command Centre; contribution is ₹Y below last week and the ✦ rail shows today's act-on-these, severity-ordered. *Drill:* hover the headline — "~70% of the gap traces to returns on three SKU clusters". *Act:* open the costliest Signal. (Triage only; no action fires from S1.)
- **Beat 1 · The wedge (S2 · T2-02).** *Hook:* "₹6.0L recoverable on the Fashion returns spike" `[illustrative]`. *Drill:* decomposition signature — 64% buyer-intent held out, the residual "chest narrow vs chart" cluster is a fixable sizing-chart error. *Act-now:* **Draft PIM sizing-chart fix** → approve → audit-logged. *Why first:* it is the most differentiating and the most MVP-feasible — returns reframed as a content problem in the customer's words.
- **Beat 2 · The seller board (S3 · T2-07).** *Hook:* "3 sellers putting ₹Y of category GMV at risk". *Drill:* portfolio→contributor — a seller whose ODR is "near the line" but whose "cancelled after I waited 3 days" clusters and 23% concentration band put it top. *Act-now:* **Draft seller coaching** (FDI-non-discrimination-aware, 25%-cap-checked) → approve → audit-logged.
- **Beat 3 · The org-fight, resolved (S4 · T2-26).** *Hook:* "Metro lane RTO 33% vs a 21% band — the voice says logistics, not seller". *Drill:* adjudication — 70% delivery-theme voice. *Act-now:* **Route to Operations**, the seller penalty held, **and** route the pick/pack process gap to the warehouse map (the diamond). *Why here:* it shows LiSN settling a fight a transaction-only tool cannot.
- **Beat 4 · The festival peak (S5 · T2-28).** *Hook:* "3× order spike — real demand or a payment failure?". *Drill:* verification — the spike vs the sale-scaled baseline + "payment deducted, no order" corroboration + an aligned account signal; **and** the suppressed near-miss beside it (a 4× apparel spike with no failure voice → expected demand, suppressed). *Act-now:* **Prepare incident packet → route to Trust & Safety + Operations** (verified, human-gated).
- **Beat 5 · The reveal (coming next).** The parked external/social-voice join shown as a teaser, not a working screen — the operational-anomaly ↔ unkeyed-voice join that extends every prior beat. Confirms the roadmap without over-claiming v1.

## 6. Interaction states (sales-demo: happy path fully built; others stubbed but never breaking the walk-through)

- **Selection / persona.** Default persona Priya (Category Head). A switch to the CX/VoC Head **re-ranks the rail** and **flips the dual-action ordering** (§3). The live rail and Day-Generator **must reset cleanly on the switch** — clear all timers/closures (the known stale-closure / timer-leak bug class); no card from the prior persona persists.
- **Filtering.** Rail severity filter (S1>S2>S3); the OwnershipBoard card-type filter bar (S3); category/lane scope selector. Filters are visual re-ranks over the seeded set — they never fetch.
- **Loading.** Static skeletons on the rail and drills; no spinner that stalls the walk-through.
- **Empty.** A static "no Signals above threshold this window" with **one suppressed near-miss visible** for credibility — never a blank screen mid-demo.
- **Error.** Static, non-blocking. **No browser storage** (artifact constraint); all state in memory for the session.
- **Simulated "live" behaviour.** The rail streams the act-on-these and the Day-Generator re-ranks; both are deterministic over the Stage 9A/9B seed so numbers tie out on every replay.

## 7. Human-gate & maker-checker moments

- **Every action is draft → human approves → audit-logged.** The approve step shows **"accepted by Priya Nair on <date>"**; the audit entry is immutable. No button reads "Complete Now" or implies autonomous firing — labels are **Draft / Prepare / Route** only (boundary; hard fail if violated).
- **Gate *before* drafting** for sensitive actions. Any seller-facing draft (coaching/review on S3) passes an **FDI non-discrimination check + 25%-concentration check**; any pin-code/geography or COD action passes the **O-9 protected-attribute guardrail** — both are drafted, never fired, and the gate is shown.
- **Advisory items carry no action button.** A Low-confidence Signal (C-9) reads "advisory — watch" and is gated from drafting an action at all, so the demo never offers an action the evidence cannot support.
- **Not a maker-checker assurance product.** There is no L1→L2→approver chain here (that pattern belongs to the SWIFT/RBI modules); the assurance model for this persona is the **draft + human gate + audit log** above, applied to every routed action.
