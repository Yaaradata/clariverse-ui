## Section B What data to collect and analyse

The panel is unanimous on one point: the value is not in collecting “more calls.” It is in making the **minimum lawful join** between interaction evidence and book outcomes. The architect and PM both argue for a strict boundary: LiSN should own the interaction corpus, consume book/event feeds, never own the lakehouse, stay cohort-first by default, never auto-fire actions, and AI-mark every generated output. The regulatory adviser reinforces that this is the only safe approach under live suitability, grievance and data-protection expectations. citeturn34view0turn25view1turn26search12turn37search4

**B1 · RM data.** This bucket includes RM–client advisory/review calls, RM free-text CRM notes, proposal-generation usage and outputs, calendarized review activity, pipeline status, productivity measures and training/coaching traces. Nuvama has already disclosed AI on proposal generation, prospect intelligence, lead scoring, advisory coaching and RM workflow, and the company’s earlier Wealth platform materials explicitly described CRM and sales management, portfolio solutions, reporting and digital servicing on one platform. What this data can reveal, once joined, is not just “which RM is active,” but which advisory behaviours correlate with mandate conversion, MPIS flows, rebalancing acceptance, or later grievance risk. The architect’s caveat is that free-text notes are often sparse and back-filled; they are useful as corroborative evidence, not ground truth. citeturn34view0turn36search5turn28view3turn29view0

**B2 · Wealth-team and partner-channel data.** This includes EWM channel data, region/branch/team structures, team books, NNM by team, RM/EWM productivity, onboarding velocity, attrition, and manager hierarchy. Nuvama’s disclosed Wealth model depends heavily on RM and EWM distribution, with late-2024/early-2025 company materials confirming 1,200+ RMs, ~7,000 active EWMs, and 450+ locations. This bucket is what lets LiSN move from “a conversation insight exists” to “the same pattern is clustering in a branch, market-head cohort or partner sub-channel.” For a 1,200-RM / 7,000-partner estate, this is the trustworthiness test: an alert is useful only if it persists across cohorts and time, not because one transcript looked worrying. citeturn36search5turn36search2turn10view0

**The join that matters most.** The winning join is simple to say and hard for incumbents to make well: **what the client said ↔ what happened to their money and the RM’s book**.
