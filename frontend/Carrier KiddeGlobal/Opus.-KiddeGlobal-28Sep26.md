# LiSN × Kidde Global Solutions (Global Commercial Fire): Prospect and Industry Research Report

Kartik Kumar runs one business, not two. His Carrier "President, Global Commercial Fire" entry is the same unit before the carve-out; Carrier sold all of its commercial and residential fire assets to Lone Star Funds on 2 December 2024.\[1\]\[2\] That business is Kidde Global Solutions (KGS), which Lone Star owns.\[3\] LiSN should pitch it as the connecting layer between the voices of installers and distributors and data on product, firmware and batch. KGS needs that layer to protect margin, installed-base revenue and its listings during the 24–36 months after the carve-out.

## TL;DR
- **One company, a PE-owned carve-out in year two.** KGS was formed on 2 December 2024 when Lone Star bought Carrier's Commercial and Residential Fire business for an enterprise value of $3 billion. Kartik Kumar leads the Global Commercial Fire segment (Edwards, Kidde Commercial, GST, Aritech, EMS, AirSense), which he puts at "~$1B+". His Carrier LinkedIn entry is stale. The interactions that matter are B2B: installers and dealers calling about panel programming, device mapping, EST3→EST4 migration, certification, orders and RMAs. Consumer volume and the scale of past recalls sit in the Residential segment.
- **The unserved need is the join, not more listening.** No named incumbent tool connects installer tech-support contacts to firmware, detector model, batch, region and distributor. That join would show a product or firmware problem, a gap left by carve-out friction, or a channel partner drifting towards Honeywell or JCI weeks before it reaches warranty cost, backorders or lost share. Regulation is making the join more valuable: the EU Cyber Resilience Act reporting duties (from 11 Sep 2026), CPSC Section 15, and UL 268 7th edition nuisance-alarm expectations.
- **Lead the pitch with "Installed-Base Early Warning", not a CX dashboard.** Put it in Kartik's P&L and Six Sigma language: *"Every week, your installers tell your tech-support line which panel, device and firmware is about to cost you margin, and nobody joins it to the batch, the region or the distributor."* Open with a one-screen demo of a Commercial Fire signal map on illustrative data, then ask for a bounded Discovery on one region's tech-support and RMA data. The 300K-per-day scale claim is irrelevant here: Commercial Fire volume is probably in the low hundreds of thousands of interactions a year [estimate].

### A.2 Drivers (with evidence)
- **UL 268 7th / UL 217 8th** took effect on 30 Jun 2024. New polyurethane fire tests and a cooking-nuisance ("hamburger") test mean most 6th-edition detectors would not pass (Hochiki).\[66\]\[67\] NFPA 72-2025 applies from 1 Jan 2025.\[44\] NFPA's "Smoke Alarms in U.S. Home Fires" (Ahrens, Mar 2014), citing a September 2010 Harris Poll, reports "Almost three-quarters (73%) said the activation was due to cooking"; this is a household survey, not measured commercial detector data. The result is a detector replacement cycle and a push towards multi-criteria designs (NA/Global).\[68\]
- **Platform migration:** EST3 new orders stopped 31 Dec 2023 and EST4 replaces it (component end-of-life). Wiring and most rail modules are backward compatible, so migration is serviceable aftermarket (NA).\[36\]\[37\]\[50\]
- **Connected/cloud panels:** EST4 + ConnectedSafety+,\[69\] KESMobile; fire panels now "live on the network" (facpmanuals).\[20\]\[50\]
- **Kumar's verticals:** energy storage (lithium-ion), petrochemicals, rail, electronics, data centres (China/Global).\[7\]
- **Mid-market:** Edge and Evolve (NA).\[8\]\[9\]\[38\] **Wireless:** EMS; 7.7–9.3% growth (UK-EU/Global).

### A.3 Competitors (Global)
- **Honeywell:** Notifier, Gamewell-FCI, Fire-Lite, Silent Knight, ESSER, Morley, Xtralis/VESDA. Edwards trains partners on VESDA,\[35\] so KGS partners also handle a competitor's aspiration product.
- **Others:** JCI (Simplex, Tyco, Zettler); Siemens (Cerberus); Bosch; Halma (Apollo, Advanced); Hochiki; Potter; Mircom; Nittan; Eaton (Cooper/JSB/Menvier); Gentex; Consilium; Chinese domestic rivals to GST.
- **M&A:** Honeywell completed its purchase of Access Solutions on 3 Jun 2024 "for $4.95 billion" (Honeywell 8-K); Sentinel bought Industrial Fire;\[12\] Pacific Avenue bought KFI.\[70\]

### A.4 Route to market
- **NA:** Edwards Strategic Partners are engineered-systems dealers with factory-trained, NICET-certified technicians covering design through ITM.\[36\]\[41\] Kidde Commercial sells through dealers.\[9\] EST4 certification takes 5 days plus eLearning and gates webinar materials and ITM courses (NFPA 72-2022 §10.5.3).\[15\]\[35\] Territories are implied by the regional sales structure [inference].
- **UK-EU:** Aritech/Kidde Commercial, EMS, AirSense via distributors and approved installers (BAFE/FIA) [inferred].
- **MEA/APAC/India:** project channels and SIs. India also has a grey/parallel market (IndiaMART) and independent EST3/Notifier board repairers.\[32\]
- **China:** GST domestic model; Edwards localisation.\[7\]
