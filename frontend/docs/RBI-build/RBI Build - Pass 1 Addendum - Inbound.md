\## Section 6 (strongest signals) — addendum

Add these inbound-specific signals to the parent Section 6 list:

\- \*\*IVR-induced frustration markers\*\* — language at agent-pickup indicating menu loops, language-switch failures, dark-pattern routing complaints.

\- \*\*Inbound queue-conduct signals\*\* — pre-abandonment customer frustration where captured in queue audio; transfer-loop reconstruction; 24×7 fraud-line operational treatment quality.

\- \*\*Repeat-contact as conduct evidence\*\* — same-customer same-issue clustering across inbound contacts within 7/14/30-day windows.

\- \*\*First-90-seconds complaint-handling adherence\*\* — agent acknowledgement, SR-creation, escalation-route disclosure (RBI Ombudsman mention), regulatory-timeline communication when a customer expresses a complaint on an inbound call. \- \*\*Vulnerable signals on general queue\*\* — distress, bereavement, fraud-victim, PwD, MSE markers detected at queue entry before specialist routing.

\- \*\*Inbound exit-handling dissuasion\*\* — retention pressure, repeated objections to customer exit request, cross-sell over exit, prolonged handling on cooling-off invocation.
