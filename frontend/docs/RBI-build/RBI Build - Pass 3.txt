# Fluid CX — RBI Conduct Intelligence: Mock-Data Model Pack

Compact entity model supporting the spine **Regulation → Obligation → Signal → Owner → Evidence → Action.** Fictional Indian Private Bank context throughout — "Suvarna Bank" (private bank, ~600 branches, mixed in-house + BPO contact centre, retail + SME + bancassurance + wealth). All sample data fictitious.

---

## 1. Regulation

**Purpose:** The regulatory anchor for every obligation. Drives "why this matters" framing in dashboard tooltips and reports.

**Key fields:**
- `regulationId` (PK)
- `shortName` — display name
- `circularRef` — RBI/SEBI/IRDAI/MeitY reference number
- `issuingBody` — RBI | SEBI | IRDAI | MeitY
- `dateIssued`
- `effectiveDate`
- `status` — `IN_FORCE` | `DRAFT_PROPOSED` | `SUPERVISORY_SIGNAL` | `ADVISORY`
- `instrumentType` — `MasterDirection` | `Circular` | `Directions` | `Scheme` | `Act` | `Rules` | `Report` | `PolicyStatement`
- `scopeApplicable` — `CommercialBanks` | `NBFCs` | `Both` | `Bancassurance` | `IAOnly`
- `relevantObligationIds` (FK array → Obligation)

**Sample records:**

```
REG-001 | "RBC Directions 2025" | RBI/DOR/2025-26/170 | RBI | 2025-11-28 | 2025-11-28 | IN_FORCE | MasterDirection | CommercialBanks | [OBL-001, OBL-003, OBL-014, OBL-015, OBL-016, OBL-019, OBL-028, OBL-030]

REG-002 | "Internal Ombudsman Directions 2026" | RBI/CEPD/2025-26/381 | RBI | 2026-01-14 | 2026-06-30 | IN_FORCE | MasterDirection | CommercialBanks | [OBL-020, OBL-021, OBL-022, OBL-023, OBL-026]

REG-003 | "RB-IOS 2026" | Press Release 2025-2026/1936 | RBI | 2026-01-16 | 2026-07-01 | IN_FORCE | Scheme | Both | [OBL-020, OBL-025]

REG-004 | "Authentication Mechanisms Directions 2025" | RBI/2025-26/79 | RBI | 2025-09-25 | 2026-04-01 | IN_FORCE | Directions | Both | [OBL-033, OBL-034, OBL-035]

REG-005 | "Draft Uniform Recovery Framework" | Draft (rev. mid-May 2026) | RBI | 2026-02-12 | 2026-07-01 (proposed) | DRAFT_PROPOSED | Directions | Both | [OBL-007, OBL-008, OBL-009, OBL-010, OBL-011, OBL-012, OBL-013]
```

**Dashboard use:** Tooltip on every obligation card; filter by issuer/status; deadline countdowns derive from `effectiveDate`; "Draft" badge applied when `status = DRAFT_PROPOSED`.

## 2. Obligation

**Purpose:** The atomic unit of conduct compliance — the link between regulation and what Fluid actually monitors.

**Key fields:**
- `oblId` (PK, format `OBL-NNN`)
- `obligationStatement` — one-line plain English
- `themeId` (FK → ConductTheme)
- `parentRegulationId` (FK → Regulation)
- `effectiveDate`
- `status` — `IN_FORCE` | `DRAFT_PROPOSED`
- `buildTier` — `MAIN_FEATURE` | `INTEGRATION_DEPENDENT` | `EVIDENCE_ONLY` | `OUT_OF_SCOPE`
- `accountableExecutivePersonaId` (FK → ExecutivePersona)
- `businessProcessOwnerRole` — string (e.g. "Head of Collections")
- `vulnerableCustomerFlag` — boolean
- `interactionSurfaces` — array of strings
- `branchDependentFlag` — boolean

**Sample records:**

```
OBL-020 | "Capture every customer complaint expressed in any channel into CMS" | THM-04 | REG-002 | 2026-06-30 | IN_FORCE | MAIN_FEATURE | L3 | "Head of Customer Service" | true | ["inbound_call","chat","email","social","ticket"] | false

OBL-010 | "Recovery agents must maintain civil tone; no threats, harassment, public shaming" | THM-02 | REG-005 | 2026-07-01 | DRAFT_PROPOSED | MAIN_FEATURE | L3 | "Head of Collections" | true | ["recovery_call"] | false

OBL-027 | "Bereaved next-of-kin claims to be handled with empathy and timely settlement" | THM-05 | REG-001 (Deceased Customers Directions, REG-007) | 2026-03-31 | IN_FORCE | MAIN_FEATURE | L2 | "Head of Customer Service" | true | ["inbound_call","branch_visit"] | true

OBL-013 | "Data minimisation and audit for borrower information shared with recovery agents" | THM-02 | REG-005 | 2026-07-01 | DRAFT_PROPOSED | OUT_OF_SCOPE | L4 | "Head of Vendor Management" | true | ["case_allocation_feed"] | false
```

**Dashboard use:** The primary navigation entity — every panel, alert, and report scopes to one or more obligations. `buildTier` drives whether the obligation appears as a main monitor, a co-control card, an evidence shelf entry, or a Do-Not-Build banner.

## 4. CustomerInteractionSignal

**Purpose:** The atomic unit of "what Fluid heard" — the raw signal feeding every alert, evidence package, and cluster.

**Key fields:**
- `signalId` (PK)
- `interactionId` (FK → interaction master)
- `interactionType` — `recovery_call` | `sales_call` | `service_call` | `fraud_dispute_call` | `chat` | `email` | `social` | `ticket` | `welcome_call` | `branch_visit`
- `channel` — voice | text | social | branch
- `signalType` — taxonomy (see below)
- `severity` — `LOW` | `MEDIUM` | `HIGH` | `CRITICAL`
- `transcriptSnippet` — short quoted segment, redacted
- `timestamp`
- `agentId` (FK → Agent)
- `vendorId` (FK → Vendor, nullable for in-house)
- `customerSegment` — `retail` | `MSE` | `wealth` | `NRI` | `bancassurance`
- `productCode` — Cards | PL | HL | Auto | SavAcct | ULIP | MF | TermDeposit
- `language` — `en` | `hi` | `ta` | `te` | `kn` | `mr` | `bn` | `gu` | `pa` | etc.
- `relatedObligationIds` (FK array → Obligation)
- `relatedAlertId` (FK → RiskAlert, nullable)

**Signal taxonomy (sample):** `threat_language`, `profanity`, `harassment_pattern`, `public_shaming`, `customer_distress`, `bundling_pressure`, `consent_extraction`, `script_deviation`, `missing_disclosure`, `banned_phrase_penal_interest`, `non_borrower_contact`, `empathy_failure`, `language_mismatch`, `complaint_marker_no_SR`, `forced_KYC_gesture`, `pre_payment_charge_quoted`, `refused_compensation_weak_auth`.

SIG-019112 | INT-10131 | recovery_call | voice | customer_distress + empathy_failure | CRITICAL | Customer: "...mere husband ka abhi operation hua hai...". Agent: "Madam EMI toh deni hi padegi..." | 2026-05-24 10:03 | AGT-1342 | VEN-001 (Sutherland Chennai) | retail | PL | hi | [OBL-011, OBL-010] | ALT-3372

## 6. EvidenceItem

**Purpose:** The proof artefact — the dashboard's bridge from "we detected something" to "here's why the supervisor will accept this".

**Key fields:**
- `evidenceId` (PK)
- `evidenceType` — `transcript_snippet` | `call_recording_clip` | `chat_excerpt` | `email_text` | `CRM_field_snapshot` | `dialer_metadata` | `journey_reconstruction` | `cluster_proof`
- `obligationId` (FK → Obligation)
- `linkedSignalIds` (FK array)
- `linkedAlertId` (FK, nullable)
- `interactionRef` — interaction ID, agent, vendor, timestamp
- `snippet` — quoted transcript text or referenced artefact
- `whyThisIsEvidence` — one-line plain explanation
- `attestationReady` — boolean (passes audit standard)
- `auditTrailId` — immutable reference

**Sample records:**

**Dashboard use:** Drilldown from every alert; attaches to CMS cases (OBL-020 feed); becomes the "Evidence pack" in IO referrals and PNO RB-IOS responses; supports quarterly RCA board pack.

## Entity relationship summary (paste-ready for Sonnet)

- **Regulation** has many **Obligation** (1:M via `parentRegulationId`)
- **Obligation** belongs to one **ConductTheme** (M:1 via `themeId`), assigned to one **ExecutivePersona** (M:1 via `accountableExecutivePersonaId`), owned by one or more **ControlOwner** (M:M)
- **CustomerInteractionSignal** links to many **Obligation** (M:M via `relatedObligationIds`)
- **RiskAlert** aggregates many **CustomerInteractionSignal** (1:M) for one **Obligation**, routed to one **ControlOwner**
- **EvidenceItem** belongs to one **Obligation** and zero or one **RiskAlert**, sources from many **CustomerInteractionSignal**
- **RCACluster** aggregates many **CustomerInteractionSignal** across one or more **Obligation**; outputs to **ExecutivePersona** L4 (CSCB pack)
- **VendorBPOScore** aggregates conduct signals scoped to one vendor across many **Obligation**
- **RecoveryConductSignal**, **ComplaintCaptureSignal**, **FraudDisputeSignal** are specialised projections of **CustomerInteractionSignal** for the three highest-leverage themes
- **IntegrationDependency** links external systems to **Obligation** carrying the `INTEGRATION_DEPENDENT` build tier
- **CapabilityBoundary** marks **Obligation** with build tier `EVIDENCE_ONLY` or `OUT_OF_SCOPE`
