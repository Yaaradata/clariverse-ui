## PERSONA 1: HEAD OF BUSINESS, SETU FASTAG (HoB)

### WHERE THEY SIT
- Reports to the Group Head, Cards & Payments at Vahan Bank; ultimately accountable to the MD & CEO on any RBI / NPCI / IHMCL board-level escalation. Carries the title "Business Head — Setu FASTag" at SVP/EVP grade per Stage 0 §3.1. P&L owns Vahan Bank's 18 million live tags, 800,000 monthly issuance, and 22 lakh daily NETC throughput.
- Daily stakeholders: Customer Operations Head (peer, the COH below — runs the contact centre and BPO governance over Trinetra Hyderabad, Anandam Coimbatore, DigitalReach Bengaluru), Principal Nodal Officer (PNO) for FASTag, Internal Ombudsman office, Compliance, Risk & Fraud, NPCI relationship manager, IHMCL nodal officer, Tech/CIO office, Marketing, channel heads (OEM, dealer, e-com, branch banking), and the corporate FASTag desk lead.
- Touches conversation data almost entirely **through intermediaries**: BPO QA dashboards from Trinetra/Anandam, MIS extracts from the Customer Operations team, Salesforce case summaries, social-listening reports from Marketing, the PNO's escalation desk. Does not listen to raw calls. Has never seen 100% of any week's conversation data. Sees the 1–3% the bank QA-samples [INFERRED, anchored to Stage 0 §5.1 / Creovai-via-Solidroad baseline].

### MENTAL MODEL
The customer base looks calm in the MIS pack and chaotic in the WhatsApp groups, and the truth is somewhere in the middle that I can't see in time to act on. The Paytm wind-down gave me an inherited share — now I have 90 days to make it permanent before IDFC FIRST or HDFC out-execute me on Annual Pass cross-sell and partner-channel quality. IDFC FIRST is publicly claiming 38% issuance-spend share in their Q1 FY26 deck; that's the number my CEO will quote at me in the next review. My costs sit in customer service and dispute leakage; that's where this should pay for itself by Q3. The thing that wakes me up at night is RB-IOS 2026 on 1 July — ₹30 lakh consequential-loss cap per case — and I'm signing off on the IO desk's readiness pack without being confident my evidence trail on the 300 oldest open complaints would survive an IO review. A good week is one where the morning social check is quiet, the chargeback win rate doesn't slip, and no one from Compliance or the IO office walks into my room with a file. A bad week is one where I find out about a plaza-level pattern on Twitter at 8:15am from my CEO's PA.

4. **Build the Annual Pass cross-sell engine inside inbound conversations.** 42 lakh passes activated nationally by Dec 2025 (Gadkari Rajya Sabha reply, Stage 0 §1); Setu FASTag's share of that needs to grow inside the inbound channel without adding headcount.

### QUESTIONS THEY CANNOT ANSWER TODAY
1. "My new-issuance numbers look strong on the channel-mix report, but how many of those OEM-fitted tags become dispute calls in the first 30 days — and is the channel partner the actual root cause?"
2. "When a customer says on a call 'I'll go to the RBI Ombudsman' or 'I'm calling 14448' — how many actually do? And what did my agent say in the 90 seconds *before* that threat?"

7. "Customers churning to IDFC / ICICI / Axis — is the churn signal sitting in their conversations with my agents weeks before the actual tag-closure call? What language are they using right before they leave?"
8. "Saksham Recovery — are they staying inside RBI's conduct expectations on every call to my negative-balance customers, or am I going to read about one of them on Twitter next month?"

### DAILY FRICTION — WHERE THEY ARE FLYING BLIND

**1. 7:30am — the social media scan**
- *Situation:* Scanning WhatsApp groups and X/Twitter for any overnight FASTag flare-up.
- *Missing signal:* Whether the angry tweet is part of a pattern (one acquirer, one plaza, one shift) or a single irate customer.
- *Current workaround:* Manual scrolling; pings the Marketing social-listening person; calls the PNO if it looks bad.
- *Lag:* 60–90 minutes to triangulate one tweet against a complaint pattern.
- *Cost of the gap:* Either over-reacts (escalates a non-pattern to the CEO at 8am) or under-reacts (misses an emerging pattern that becomes a board-level fire by 11am).

**2. 8:30am — the MIS pack**
- *Situation:* Reviewing the prior-day pack on issuance by channel, txn count/value, DA ratio, complaint counts by category, NPS pulse, tech incidents.
- *Missing signal:* What's breaking *this morning*, not yesterday. Whether yesterday's complaint spike is decelerating or accelerating.
- *Current workaround:* T-1 numbers in the MIS pack, T-2 on a few lagging fields.
- *Lag:* 24–48 hours behind reality.
- *Cost of the gap:* The 10am ops huddle is run on yesterday's reality, not today's.

**3. 10:00am — the ops huddle**
- *Situation:* Cross-functional huddle with Tech, COH, BPO partner lead, Risk, Marketing.
- *Missing signal:* What is the contact centre *actually* hearing this morning? Which of the top-15 complaint categories is moving and why?
- *Current workaround:* Verbal reports from BPO supervisors at Trinetra and Anandam; numbers from yesterday.
- *Lag:* Anecdotal in the room; quantification arrives 24h later.
- *Cost of the gap:* Decisions on where to deploy floor capacity, where to push Tech tickets, where to fire Marketing comms — all on hunch.

**4. 14:30 — the customer-service deep-dive**
- *Situation:* Weekly deep-dive with COH and BPO governance on complaints by category, refund TAT, IO queue, BPO QA scores.
- *Missing signal:* Whether the 1–3% QA sample is representative of the 97% the bank never listens to.
- *Current workaround:* Trust the QA rubric and the BPO governance contract; spot-check.
- *Lag:* Permanent — the question never closes.
- *Cost of the gap:* Decisions on BPO performance, agent retraining, IVR/FAQ updates are all made on a sample that may not reflect the real conversation stream.

**5. 18:00 — drafting the PNO / IO response**
- *Situation:* Signing off on the Principal Nodal Officer's correspondence on a media-escalated grievance, or reviewing a draft response to an Internal Ombudsman case.
- *Missing signal:* The full conversation trail for that specific customer — calls, chats, emails, what was promised, what was delivered.
- *Current workaround:* Salesforce case summary, manual pulls from the call-recording archive, BPO supervisor's account.
- *Lag:* 2–4 hours per case for a clean assembly; sometimes incomplete.
- *Cost of the gap:* Responses that omit a relevant interaction; settlement offers calibrated wrong; IO findings against the bank that could have been avoided.

**6. End of day — separating the viral one-off from the real pattern**
- *Situation:* A viral X post from a known auto journalist about a wrong deduction at a Karnataka plaza.
- *Missing signal:* Whether this is one customer one event, or part of a 50-call cluster over the last 72 hours.
- *Current workaround:* Asks COH and Marketing to triangulate by morning.
- *Lag:* Overnight.
- *Cost of the gap:* Either authorises a defensive comms response that draws more attention, or stays silent on a real pattern that grows by morning.

### ANTI-PATTERNS — WHAT MUST NOT BE ON THIS PERSONA'S DASHBOARD
1. **Agent-level surveillance metrics.** A leaderboard ranking individual Trinetra or Anandam agents by sentiment score erodes BPO governance trust and breaks the contract Vahan Bank holds with its outsourced partners. HoB does not manage agents directly — that's COH's surface, and even there it's coaching, not surveillance.
2. **Vanity counters with no decision attached.** "Total interactions analysed today: 64,983" is not a number HoB will act on. Every tile must point to a decision, an owner, and a time-to-act.
3. **Sentiment-only alerts without provenance.** A "negative sentiment spike" alert with no link to the underlying calls, no historical baseline, and no source-channel breakdown will be ignored after the second false-positive.
4. **Anything that looks like Fluid CX deciding chargeback merits, recovery action, or IO case outcomes.** Per the §4 product boundary contract — HoB will not action a signal that depends on the platform replacing NPCI dispute switch logic, Saksham's recovery workflow, or the Internal Ombudsman's case judgement.

### TRUST THRESHOLD — WHAT THIS PERSONA NEEDS TO SEE TO ACT ON A SIGNAL
1. **One-click drill-down to the underlying interactions.** Every alert, every trend line, every action-queue item must let HoB reach the actual call recordings, chat transcripts, emails or social posts that triggered the signal in under three seconds. Senior bankers do not act on opaque AI — they act on evidence.
2. **Explicit baseline framing.** Every signal must show "this is 2.4x the trailing 8-week median for this category at this hour" — not just "high". HoB needs to know what they're looking at is genuinely anomalous before they call the CEO, the PNO, or NPCI.
3. **Named downstream owner on every action.** No orphan signals. Every row must say which role acts on it — COH, BPO supervisor at Trinetra, NPCI dispute team, Marketing, Tech, IO office — so HoB can route, not absorb.

### HOW THEIR PERFORMANCE IS JUDGED
1. **Tag-in-force and net new-issuance share** — Lagging today (monthly MIS, NPCI ecosystem stats). Conversation data improves the leading indicator by surfacing channel-quality signal (Day-1 OEM/dealer-issuance complaints) weeks before they hit the issuance-share number.
2. **Net chargeback ratio / chargeback win rate** — Near-real-time today only at aggregate level via NPCI dispute system. Conversation data tightens the gap by flagging evidence-thin disputes at the moment of the call, before they are filed.
3. **Customer-service cost per active tag** — Lagging (monthly). Conversation data unlocks the deflectable-call analysis (balance check, simple recharge) that drives the cost-out plan.
4. **RBI Ombudsman case openings against the bank** — Lagging (monthly RBI report; ORBIO disposal 93.07% systemwide per Stage 0 §7). Conversation data provides the live "customer threatens Ombudsman" early-warning — the single highest-leverage early indicator.
5. **IO desk findings and partial-resolution rate** — Lagging (quarterly under IO Directions 2026 clause-7 series). Conversation data builds the IO-defensible evidence trail required under the 30 June 2026 clauses.
6. **Annual Pass cross-sell uptake from inbound conversations** — Currently not measured. Conversation data is the only way to measure this — there is no other data source for the missed-prompt count.

### WHAT THIS PERSONA NEEDS FROM THEIR FLUID CX DASHBOARD
1. **"...see at 8:30am, *before* my ops huddle, which 3 complaint categories grew fastest in the last 12 hours, with one-line evidence — actual customer phrases — so I can walk into the 10am meeting knowing what to push on, not asking what happened."**
2. **"...tell within an hour of a Twitter spike whether it is a real cross-plaza pattern I need to call the PNO and IHMCL about, or a single irate customer I should leave alone."**
3. **"...defend my chargeback win rate to my Group Head with named evidence on which dispute reason codes we're losing on, which agent cohorts file evidence-thin packs, and which plazas drive the rejected chargebacks."**
4. **"...show the IO desk on 30 June that every partially-resolved complaint in the queue has a complete, time-stamped evidence trail assembled from voice + chat + email + complaint — before they ask."**
5. **"...spot, every shift, the auto-recharge-opt-in and Annual Pass cross-sell conversations my agents missed — so Marketing and Tech can fix the IVR prompt and the script before the afternoon toll rush."**

## PERSONA 2: CUSTOMER OPERATIONS HEAD, SETU FASTAG (COH)

### WHERE THEY SIT
- Reports to the FASTag Business Head (HoB above) on a solid line and to the bank-wide Head of Customer Service on a dotted line. Owns the contact-centre P&L for Setu FASTag, BPO governance over Trinetra BPO Hyderabad (primary voice), Anandam Customer Solutions Coimbatore (secondary voice), and DigitalReach Bengaluru (chat + social). Carries Salesforce Financial Services Cloud as the CRM of record and Genesys Cloud + Ozonetel as the CCaaS layer.

- Touches conversation data more directly than HoB but still through intermediaries: Genesys Cloud reporting, BPO supervisor narratives, Salesforce case audits, 1–3% QA sample reviews, ad-hoc call pulls when a case escalates. Has *never* had 100% of the conversation stream available as analysed signal. Operates on what's escalated up and what's spot-checked down.

### MENTAL MODEL
My floor handles 65,000 FASTag interactions a day across voice, chat, email, social and 1033-routed cases, and I genuinely only know what's happening on 1–3% of them. My MIS pack tells me yesterday's category counts; my BPO supervisors tell me what feels hot; my Salesforce queue tells me what's already broken. What I cannot see is the *pattern in the middle* — the 60-call cluster that's the early sign of a plaza-level outage, the agent-script drift that explains why my FCR looks fine but my repeat-call rate is creeping up, the Saksham recovery call that's two notches over the line. A good week is one where I can defend my BPO partners against the HoB's "what's happening" questions with named evidence, not anecdotes, and where I've closed the OC 005 evidence-upload window on every disputed transaction. A bad week is one where the IO desk finds a partial-resolution case I didn't know was at risk, or HoB hears about a plaza pattern from social listening before I do from my own floor.

### WHAT THEY ARE TRYING TO MOVE IN THE NEXT 90 DAYS

4. **Build the IO-defensible evidence trail on every open complaint by 30 June.** This is the single biggest deliverable the IO Directions 2026 places on the contact-centre operation, and it is COH's surface to defend, not HoB's.
5. **Reduce 1033 inbound forwarded volume by fixing the top-3 plaza-level leakage patterns** in collaboration with HoB and the NPCI dispute team — measured by 1033 forwards / 10,000 transactions.

- *Situation:* Weekly review of chargebacks filed, rejected under 5225 or auto-rejected under 3207, won, and pending.

- *Missing signal:* Which cases will actually become IO findings vs. which are noise; which have evidence gaps that need to be filled before they reach IO.
- *Current workaround:* Case-by-case review; relies on case-handler's tagging.

**6. Weekly — the Saksham Recovery conduct review**
- *Situation:* Sample listening to 20–30 recovery calls per week to negative-balance customers.
- *Missing signal:* The other 99% of recovery calls — whether any crossed the conduct line.
- *Current workaround:* Random sample; trust in Saksham's training; hope.
- *Lag:* Could be never — a problematic call may never be sampled.
- *Cost of the gap:* Reputational exposure on a conduct failure that surfaces only when a customer goes to the press or RBI.

### ANTI-PATTERNS — WHAT MUST NOT BE ON THIS PERSONA'S DASHBOARD
1. **Agent-level sentiment leaderboards visible to anyone outside the BPO supervisor circle.** Destroys floor morale; breaks the BPO governance trust contract; counterproductive to coaching outcomes.
2. **Real-time agent-stress alerts driven by subjective AI signals** (e.g., "agent voice indicates fatigue") without explainable provenance. Will not be trusted by BPO floor leadership and will erode the dashboard's overall credibility.
3. **Alarm-volume alerts before the 8:00am queue handover.** If COH is being paged at 6am because the system fired a soft anomaly overnight, the dashboard will be muted by the end of week one.
4. **Anything that claims to assess complaint merit or recommend case outcomes.** That sits with the IO desk and Compliance; per §4 product boundary contract, Fluid CX surfaces patterns, it does not adjudicate cases.

2. **Pattern qualification — never one call, always a cohort.** Every alert must specify: "this signal fired across N interactions, at M plazas/agents/shifts, in the trailing T hours, vs. an 8-week baseline of X." Single-call alerts produce alarm fatigue.
3. **BPO-governance-friendly framing.** Coaching insights for supervisors at Trinetra/Anandam/DigitalReach — not surveillance dossiers for HoB. The dashboard must signal "what to coach", not "who to fire".

3. **FCR by category** — Lagging (monthly); conversation data converts it into a near-real-time signal by detecting same-tag callbacks within 14 days and the agent-promise-vs-delivery gap.
4. **BPO QA scores by vendor / shift** — Sample-based today (1–3%); conversation data extends it to 100% coverage and surfaces variance by named shift and vendor.
5. **IO desk escalation rate / partial-resolution rate** — Lagging (quarterly under IO Directions 2026); conversation data builds the evidence trail and surfaces at-risk cases days in advance.
6. **1033 forwarded volume / 10,000 transactions** — Lagging (monthly via IHMCL); conversation data converts plaza-level patterns into actionable signals for the NPCI dispute desk.

## PERSONA RELATIONSHIP SECTION

**Who sees a FASTag conversation signal first depends on signal class.** Live alerts — Ombudsman-threat language (FCX-FT-S006), Saksham conduct deviation (FCX-FT-S018), social spike correlated with a plaza cluster (FCX-FT-S003 + S021) — route to COH first because COH is on the floor and can act inside the hour. Trend and channel-quality signals — Day-1 OEM-issuance cohort complaints (FCX-FT-S007), Annual Pass cross-sell misses (FCX-FT-S002), churn-intent language (FCX-FT-S001), GNSS awareness (FCX-FT-S009) — route to HoB first because they speak to product, channel and strategy choices COH does not control. The handoff is bidirectional: COH escalates live alerts up to HoB when a pattern crosses a plaza-density or PNO-relevance threshold; HoB pushes channel-quality and cross-sell signals down to COH to operationalise on the floor.

**Their goals create real tension at three points.** First, growth-channel volume vs. complaint absorption — HoB pushes OEM/dealer/e-com issuance growth to defend the post-Paytm share; COH absorbs the downstream Day-1 KYV-mismatch and class-misread calls when channel quality slips. Second, BPO cost-out vs. evidence-pack quality — HoB measures cost-per-tag and pushes BPO efficiency; COH knows OC 005 evidence-pack completeness requires agent time that AHT optimisation erodes. Third, the IO defensibility deadline (30 June 2026) — both own pieces of it (HoB the strategic narrative, COH the case-by-case evidence trail), and historically each has waited for the other's pack before signing off.

**A shared intelligence surface changes the working relationship in one specific way:** both stop arguing from separate MIS packs in the Monday review. HoB walks into the 10am ops huddle having seen the same morning brief as COH; COH no longer has to defend "what the floor is actually hearing" against HoB's social-listening read; the conversation moves from "whose number is right" to "which of these three patterns do we act on first, and who owns it by end of week." The dashboard becomes the shared text of the operation.
