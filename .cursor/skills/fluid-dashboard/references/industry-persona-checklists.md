# Industry → Persona Checklists
Audit asks: "good FOR THIS industry, persona, and question?"

## Retail Banking — Head of Contact Centre
Q: are contact-centre ops resolving issues efficiently, consistently, within service expectations?
KPIs: FCR (70-85%; ~1% FCR ≈ 1% CSAT; a repeat contact can drop CSAT ~15%), AHT, CSAT (~85%+), CES, ASA, call-containment, agent attrition.
Must show: contact volume by channel · SLA/TAT · FCR · repeat-contact drivers · agent/team/queue hotspots · top intents · escalation queue · sentiment trend · evidence · action queue.
Hard fail: only call volume · no SLA/FCR/repeat-contact · no top reasons · no queue/team actionability.
Weighting: operational 25 · SLA/FCR/repeat 20 · queue/team actionability 20 · customer-pain 15 · visual clarity 10 · evidence 10.

## Retail Banking — Head of Retail Banking
Q: how is the business performing, where is growth/friction, which journeys need attention?
KPIs: CASA, NIM, loan-to-deposit ratio, NPL ratio, cross-sell ratio, digital adoption (>70%), CLV vs CAC, ROA, efficiency ratio, account-opening/loan turnaround.
Must show: product performance · growth drivers · journey friction · branch/region comparison · segment/product cuts · digital-vs-branch servicing · complaints linked to business impact · action queue.
Hard fail: reads like contact-centre ops · no business drivers · issues not linked to business impact · no product/segment/region view.
Weighting: business performance 25 · growth/friction 20 · product/segment/region 20 · journey impact 15 · actionability 10 · visual clarity 10.

## Retail Banking — Head of CX
Q: are customers getting a consistent, fair, low-friction experience across journeys/channels?
KPIs: NPS (50+; increasingly a supporting signal — pair with task-success, time-to-resolution, repeat-contact, behavioural signals), CES, CSAT/OSAT, journey-completion, FCR, retention/churn/CLV, closed-loop rate.
Must show: journey health · top pain points · sentiment · repeat contact · complaint capture+resolution · journey-stage heatmap · channel-wise experience · sensitive-customer signals (distress, hardship, bereavement, fraud victim) · AI CX insight · recovery queue.
Hard fail: only volume/SLA · no journey pain · no sentiment/repeat-issue view · no recovery/action view.
Weighting: journey-experience 25 · pain/sentiment 20 · complaint/repeat visibility 20 · cross-channel consistency 15 · recovery actions 10 · evidence 10.

## Credit Cards — Head of Credit Cards
Q: are cardholders satisfied across the journey, where are service/product issues, what hits portfolio health?
Journey (Card MD 2022/2024): application → approval → dispatch → activation → billing → dispute → closure.
Focus: billing/fee pain (late fee, annual fee, EMI conversion, reversals) · dispute lifecycle (raised → provisional credit → investigation → closure; no charge on fraud-disputed txn until resolved) · 7-working-day closure · delivery/activation friction · high-value cardholders · partner friction (co-brand, network, courier).
Hard fail: retail banking with a "card" label · ignores dispute/billing depth · no journey stages · no activation/delivery friction.
Weighting: cardholder-journey coverage 25 · billing/dispute/service 20 · business impact 15 · product/partner friction 15 · high-value visibility 10 · actionability/evidence 15.

## RBI Conduct — Head of Product / Digital
Q: are product journeys, digital flows, disclosures, communications conduct-compliant, and where are customers exposed?
Obligations (RBC Directions 2025 + 2026 amendments): explicit/separate consent per product · no compulsory bundling · suitability before recommendation · KFS/disclosure clarity · no dark patterns (false urgency, drip pricing, subscription traps, bait-and-switch) · campaign-claim accuracy · 30-day post-sale feedback.
Digital KPIs: account-opening conversion · funnel abandonment by stage · feature adoption · cost-to-serve · straight-through processing · uptime.
Must show: product obligation coverage · journey control funnel (enquiry → recommendation → disclosure → consent → post-sale) · disclosure/KFS clarity · cooling-off communication · cross-sell consent/bundling risk · suitability check · campaign-claim accuracy · digital-journey complaint signals · outbound product conduct · location/vendor conduct · release/campaign risk queue.
Hard fail: generic compliance screen · no product/digital journey · no KFS/disclosure/consent/suitability · CRO-style audit logs instead of product-owner actions.
Weighting: obligation coverage 25 · journey/design-friction 20 · disclosure/consent/suitability 20 · campaign/script/release risk 15 · owner actionability 10 · evidence 10.

## RBI Conduct — CRO / Chief Conduct Officer
Q: are conduct obligations met bank-wide, where are breaches by severity/trend, is there defensible evidence?
How the role works: KRIs vs a risk-appetite statement with limits; material breaches escalate to board + regulator; conduct risk reported to board (via Compliance); third-party/outsourcing risk in scope; needs aggregated defensible signal, not agent-level noise.
Regulatory teeth: RB-IOS 2026 (entity responds in 15 days; 90-day escalation window; up to ₹30 lakh consequential + ₹3 lakh harassment; "deficiency in service" standard); unauthorised-txn liability shifts burden to the bank, 30-day resolution.
Must show: enterprise conduct-risk score · obligation coverage (met/watch/breach/missing evidence) · high-risk obligation heatmap · business-unit/product exposure · channel/location signals · breach-severity queue · evidence readiness (transcript, timestamp, owner, audit pack) · repeat-breach themes · regulatory-reporting readiness · AI conduct brief · owner remediation tracker.
Hard fail: no obligation coverage · no severity/evidence readiness · too operational · no audit defensibility.
Weighting: enterprise conduct-risk 25 · obligation coverage + breach severity 25 · evidence/audit defensibility 20 · business-unit/product exposure 15 · owner remediation 10 · visual clarity 5.
