# Perfect Dashboard Checklist (common)
A perfect Fluid dashboard: the target executive grasps the headline in 5 seconds and the full story (what changed, why, where, who owns it, what to do) in ~2 minutes. The test is time-to-decision.

## Common checks
Business question clarity · persona fit · insight depth · spine coverage · component usefulness · visual hierarchy (headline top-left) · actionability · data quality · build quality · 5-9 KPIs (not >12).

## Scorecard /100
Business question clarity 15 · Persona fit 15 · Insight depth 15 · spine coverage 15 · component usefulness 10 · visual hierarchy 10 · actionability 10 · data quality 5 · build quality 5. (Persona weightings override when the persona is known.)

## Score bands
90-100 demo-ready · 80-89 small refinement · 70-79 not executive-ready · 60-69 major rework · <60 rebuild.

## Hard fails
Main question unanswered · persona generic · no signal/evidence layer · no action queue · decorative charts · headline not top-left · >12 KPIs · same layout as another persona · numbers fake/inconsistent.

## 7-question gut check
Who is it for · what question · what changed · why · where · who owns the action · what evidence.
